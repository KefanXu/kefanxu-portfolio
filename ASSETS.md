# Research project media

Project detail pages use documented research figures from published work or user-supplied manuscripts. Decorative folio-cover artwork is kept separate from the project documentation and is never presented as a product screenshot.

## Original Moodloop materials

The Moodloop reader uses materials supplied by Kefan for this portfolio update:

- `public/images/projects/moodloop-video-poster.jpg` is the public lead still from the [Moodloop project film](https://youtu.be/otqw3gwkwbE). The poster activates a privacy-enhanced YouTube player inside the reader; the external link remains available as a fallback.
- `public/images/projects/moodloop-phone.svg` is an export of the phone mockup in the [Moodloop Figma file](https://www.figma.com/design/4HrDos6m8q1sM7HXqmlWLT/Moodloop--Official-?node-id=2132-2305).
- `public/images/projects/moodloop-study.png` is the six-week study-design figure exported from Figma and resized to 2400 pixels on its longest edge for the web. Its content is unchanged.

The public case-study copy is grounded in the supplied manuscript. It uses the consistent six-week/42-day study duration and avoids conflicting annotation totals in the draft.

## Original Physicify materials

The Physicify reader uses three interface exports from Kefan's author-supplied [Experiment Design Figma file](https://www.figma.com/design/F5hIMzRhYagDBnYZWBBh0z/Experiment-Design?node-id=2739-83):

- `public/images/projects/physicify-history-summary.png` presents aggregate completion history by weather, activity type, weekday, and time.
- `public/images/projects/physicify-calendar-planning.png` places color-coded outcomes beside weather and anonymized schedule context.
- `public/images/projects/physicify-record-detail.png` preserves a selected plan's outcome, reason, conditions, and adjacent events.

The interface claims and study metrics were cross-checked against the author-supplied CHI 2022 paper. The reader describes the 28-day study as exploratory and does not claim that access to historical records improved adherence.
